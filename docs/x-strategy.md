# @tryskillbridge X Strategy — Pre-Launch

## Philosophy
**Be helpful first, promote never.** The account should feel like a knowledgeable friend in the career space — not a product account. Promotion comes naturally once people trust the voice.

---

## Daily Rhythm (Conservative)

| Activity | Frequency | Credits Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Original tweets | 1–2/day | ~$0.01/day |
| Replies to big accounts | 2–3/day | ~$0.02/day |
| Likes on relevant tweets | 5–10/day | ~$0.01/day |
| Quote tweets | 1 every 2–3 days | ~$0.005/day |

**Estimated daily cost: ~$0.04 → ~$1.20/month** (well within $5 budget)

## Content Pillars (Original Tweets)

### 1. Career Wisdom (40%)
Short, punchy insights about career growth. No links, no selling.
> "The biggest skill gap isn't technical — it's knowing what to learn next."

> "Nobody got promoted by learning everything. They got promoted by learning the right things at the right time."

### 2. Industry Insights (30%)
Hot takes on tech careers, hiring, and skill trends.
> "Companies say they want 'Senior Engineers' but the job description is 3 different roles. Here's how to decode it..."

### 3. Practical Tips (20%)
Actionable advice people can use immediately.
> "Before your next interview, do this:
> 1. Find 3 recent blog posts from the company's engineering team
> 2. Reference them in your answers
> 3. Watch the interviewer light up
> 
> It works because almost nobody does it."

### 4. Engagement / Community (10%)
Questions, polls, conversation starters.
> "What's the one skill you wish you'd learned 2 years earlier? 👇"

### Reply Rules (CRITICAL — anti-ban)
1. **NEVER mention SkillBridge or drop a link in replies** (instant spam flag)
2. **Add genuine value** — share an insight, personal angle, or useful tip
3. **Be conversational** — short, natural, like a real person replying
4. **No templates** — every reply must be unique and contextual
5. **Max 3 replies/day** to big accounts (X flags accounts that mass-reply)
6. **Space them out** — minimum 2 hours between replies
7. **Mix accounts** — don't reply to the same person more than 2x/week
8. **Like before replying** — feels more natural to the algorithm

### Reply Examples

**If @GergelyOrosz posts about career ladders:**
> "The jump from Senior to Staff is where most people stall. It's not about writing more code — it's about making the right code not need to be written."

**If @swyx posts about learning in public:**

**If @SahilBloom posts about career frameworks:**

## Growth Milestones

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4) — NOW
- Build a content library of 50+ tweet drafts
- Establish consistent posting rhythm
- Get first 100 followers organically
- **Zero promotion. Zero links. Just value.**

### Phase 2: Credibility (Weeks 5–8)
- Start getting replies and engagement on our tweets
- Begin occasional quote tweets of big accounts
- Pin a tweet that describes the mission (still no hard sell)
- Target: 300–500 followers

### Phase 3: Soft Launch (Weeks 9–12)
- Pin tweet with link to tryskillbridge.com
- Add website to bio
- Occasional "I built something..." tweet (1x/week max)
- Let followers discover the product naturally
- Target: 1,000 followers

## Safety Guardrails
- **Rate limit:** Never exceed 5 tweets + 5 replies + 15 likes per day
- **No automation patterns:** Vary posting times (±2 hours from schedule)
- **No hashtag spam:** Max 1-2 hashtags per tweet, often none
- **No follow/unfollow games:** Only follow accounts we genuinely want to read
- **Cool-down:** If any tweet gets flagged or engagement drops, go silent for 48 hours
- **Human review:** All replies to big accounts get logged for Stefan to review
